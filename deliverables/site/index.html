What happens when models check their own output and retry?

Hyde left this open at the end of AtelierBench. I ran it.

They fix the brief violations that validation waves through, but only when the check says how to fix them. And the check doesn't need the image: checking the workflow before it runs gets the same result as checking the render, for half the image generations.

      Most of the gap was missing information

      The agent was never told the logo's pixel size, and none of its tools reports it, so it couldn't work out where a corner placement starts. The workflow check reads the file and says it outright: x=1348, y=32.

      When I wrote the logo's size into the brief instead, validation alone met the brief in 89% of runs on the same briefs. So the order of fixes matters: give the model the facts first, then let the check catch what's left. It costs nothing to run.

      A scorer, not a judge

      Your Catan post made the case for a cheap scorer plus search. A brief contract is that scorer for design workflows: deterministic, under a millisecond, no model call.

      Asked to pick one of four sampled workflows, it chose as well as an oracle on all 73 briefs, and never rejected a workflow that actually met the brief. That makes it usable as a filter, or as a reward for training small workflow models.

      It also holds on a 4B model on a 4 GB laptop GPU: the plan check lifted qwen3.5:4b from 12% to 42% of briefs met (48 runs). Half of that model's runs never produced a workflow that executes at all, so for small models, building the graph is the first problem.

      What this doesn't show

      • Objective claims only. Size, deliverable count, logo pixels and position, reference composition. Not style, rendered text or taste: those still need judges and designers.
      • Image content is stubbed. Geometry comes from real ComfyUI nodes; what the diffusion model would have painted is not real.
      • Synthetic briefs, one model. Briefs are modelled on Exhibit 1, not your 156 tasks. DeepSeek V4.1 Flash only; no frontier model was tested.
      • A friendlier baseline than yours. My validation-only harness puts a node reference in the prompt and validates at every submit. Compare the checks with each other, not with your numbers.
      • Information versus checking. Most of the logo gain came from facts the check supplied. The follow-up that showed this was designed after seeing the first results.
